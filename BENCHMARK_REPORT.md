## Notes

- `ratio = GA_cost / UCS_cost`. A ratio of 1.0 means the GA found an optimal solution.
- The GA models the world using pure `applyGate` transitions (no shelf state, no cascading).
  The UCS baseline (`planning/solver.ts::solve`) uses the same model — both ignore shelf state,
  so the comparison is fair.
- Problems where either algorithm failed are excluded from ratio statistics but counted in
  success rates and in the failure counts.
